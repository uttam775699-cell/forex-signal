# Money Crash Files: growth plan

Owner's goal: grow the channel with a USA audience. Quality over quantity: nothing below 90/100 ships.

## Starting point (2026-11-21)
4 subscribers, 20 views, 9 Shorts. 5 of 9 are off-niche quizzes/riddles (flag quiz, emoji movie): they confuse
YouTube about who the channel is for. Recommendation given to the owner: make them private (owner's decision).

## Positioning
"Every great American money disaster, told in 50 seconds as a cartoon." Cartoon storytelling + exact facts is the edge:
the topics are popular, but almost every competing Short is stock footage + text with no story.

## Content pillars (rotate; never two of the same pillar in a row)
1. **Frauds as heists:** one person, one trick, how they got caught (Monopoly heist, Ponzi, salad-oil swindle, Madoff's sons).
2. **Forgotten crazy money stories:** little-known, absurd, true (Knight Capital's 45 minutes, the Hunt brothers' silver).
3. **Crashes in one breath:** the single cause of a crash told through one character (1929 margin calls, SVB's group chats).
4. **Corporate blunders that cost billions:** a decision you can picture (Blockbuster vs Netflix, New Coke) when competition is low.

## What every Short does
- First 2 seconds: a number + a twist ("For years, the million-dollar pieces never made it to the fries").
- 45–60 s, 14–17 scenes, a cut or camera move every 2–4 s, burned-in captions.
- One "Luckily / Unluckily" or reversal beat in the middle; the last line calls back to the hook (loops well).
- Every number from at least two reliable sources; disputed numbers become ranges ("tens of millions").
- Title ≤ 50 characters, a concrete number or name + the twist, no clickbait lies, at most one emoji.
- Description: 2 lines of story + sources + 3–5 hashtags (#shorts #moneyhistory #fraud ...). Pinned comment: a question.

## Cadence
- 1 Short a day, ready for review ~6 PM IST; owner uploads ~10:30 PM IST (= 12:00 PM ET, US lunch peak).
- Wednesday: channel audit (what got views, swipe-away, comments) -> adjust pillars, hooks and titles in this file.
- After 20 Shorts: test a 6–8 min long-form compilation of the best 8 (same visuals, new intro) for watch time.
